# Almanac v1

Almanac is a 1v1 Nemesis format. Each player builds a run by drafting decks, and from ante 3 every PvP blind is played under boss rules that both players choose and both players suffer. It inherits Expanded Ranked's baseline (content, modifiers, lives and timer) except where noted below.

- **Start.** Both players play the Almanac Deck. Ante 1 has no draft.
- **Stake draft.** Before the run, the board has five stakes on the Almanac Deck: White, Green, Black, Purple and Gold. White becomes White- at 50% (White, plus Grabber and Wasteful). One separate roll replaces either Purple with Planet (25%) or Gold with Spectral+ (25%), never both. Players alternate three bans, then the second player picks from the last two.
- **Deck draft.** At antes 2, 3, 5 and 7, both players see the same three decks, each picks one, and each keeps its pick for the rest of the run. Offers exclude every deck either player already holds.
- **Boss rules.** From ante 3, before every PvP blind, each player privately picks one of four Boss Blind effects within 10 s. Both picks apply to both players for that blind.
- **Rule resolution.** When the deadline passes, any missing pick is rolled from that player's own offer. If both players pick the same rule, a random different rule is added from the full ante-eligible pool, so every blind has two distinct rules.
- **Rule pool.** From ante 3: Manacle, Psychic, House, Club, Goad, Window, Head, Glass Cannon, Tooth and Arm. Ante 4 adds Verdant Leaf. Ante 5 adds Hook, Plant and Water.
- **Forecast.** The pause menu shows the draft schedule and status. It never reveals future offers.
- **Queue.** Almanac is a separate competitive queue. The Almanac Deck stays in practice and is excluded from the other queues.
