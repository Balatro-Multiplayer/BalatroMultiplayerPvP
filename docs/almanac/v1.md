# Almanac v1

**Almanac is a competitive queue with a mandatory Almanac deck. Ante 1 is clear skies. At antes 2, 3, 5 and 7, both players receive the same three deck offers, choose independently, and keep their acquisitions. Before PvP encounters, players draft boss-blind effects that apply to both players.**

This is the accepted product statement, not a claim that the prototype already
implements it. Remaining work is tracked in `release-checklist.md`.

## Scope

- Accumulating deck choices are the primary progression.
- Boss-effect choices affect both players for the encounter, not just the opponent.
- Almanac names the queue, its signature deck and its internal IDs.
- Previous direction retains practice access and excludes this deck from other
  competitive queues. Enforcement remains to be built.
- Every run has a forecast: schedule/status only. The pause-menu forecast is
  implemented; rendered validation remains outstanding.

## Out of scope for v1

- Temporary opportunities, Higher Calling, Everybody Counts, One of Each.
- Automatic Chaos Clock, event budgets or opportunity scheduling.
- A public mutator configuration wall or new content/balance experiments.
- No promise that all preserved experiments ship in v1.1.

## Decisions

1. Forecast: schedule/status only.
2. Shared deck pool after divergent picks: exclude any deck acquired by either
   player. Both players receive the same three eligible deck offers.
3. Boss-rule start: ante 3, then every PvP encounter after that.
4. Boss-rule offers: each player receives their own four-rule offer. This does
   not change the three-offer deck draft.
5. Fixed baseline: inherit Expanded Ranked's baseline for content, modifiers,
   stakes/sleeves, lives and timer, except for Almanac's intended differences.
6. Ante scaling: skip for now

7. Boss-rule eligibility: ante 3 offers Manacle, Psychic, House, Club, Goad,
   Window, Head, Glass Cannon, Tooth and Arm. Ante 4 adds Verdant Leaf. Ante 5
   adds Hook, Plant and Water. Earlier rules remain eligible.
8. Boss-rule deadline: ten seconds. Resolve immediately when both picks lock;
   at the deadline, randomly select from each unanswered player's own offer,
   preserving any locked pick.
9. Matching boss picks: apply the chosen rule once and add a random different
   rule from the entire ante-eligible pool, not just the offered rules. Every
   encounter receives two distinct rules.
10. Pre-run draft, for now: five stake offers with the Almanac deck fixed.
    Green, Black and Purple are always present. Independently roll a 50%
    chance to replace Gold with Spectral+, and a 50% chance to replace White
    with White-. White- uses White's rules and starts with Grabber and Wasteful
    vouchers. These replacement rolls supersede Expanded's Spectral+ offer
    weighting for Almanac.
