# Almanac v1

**Almanac is a competitive queue with a mandatory Almanac deck. Ante 1 is clear skies. At antes 2, 3, 5 and 7, both players receive the same three deck offers, choose independently, and keep their acquisitions. Before PvP encounters, players draft boss-blind effects that apply to both players.**

This is the accepted product statement, not a claim that the prototype already
implements it. Remaining work is tracked in `release-checklist.md`.

## Scope

- Accumulating deck choices are the primary progression.
- Boss-effect choices affect both players for the encounter, not just the opponent.
- Almanac names the queue, its signature deck and its internal IDs.
- Previous direction retains practice access and excludes this deck from other
  competitive queues. Enforcement remains to be built.
- Every run has a forecast: schedule/status only. The UI remains to be implemented.

## Out of scope for v1

- Temporary opportunities, Higher Calling, Everybody Counts, One of Each.
- Automatic Chaos Clock, event budgets or opportunity scheduling.
- A public mutator configuration wall or new content/balance experiments.
- No promise that all preserved experiments ship in v1.1.

## Decisions

1. Forecast: schedule/status only.
2. Shared deck pool after divergent picks: exclude either player's acquisitions
   (recommended, not approved), or another explicit repeat/eligibility rule?
3. Boss-rule start: prototype starts at ante 3. Every PvP after that.
4. Boss pool/gates, ten-second timeout, duplicate wildcard: retain prototype rules?
5. Fixed baseline: content, modifiers, stakes/sleeves, lives and timer.
6. Ante scaling: skip for now

Do not implement recommendations as settled decisions; ask before filling gaps.
