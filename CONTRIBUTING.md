# Contributing to Balatro Multiplayer

## Types of Contributions

We encourage people open PRs or issues related to the following features:
- Localization
- Quality of Life
- Bug fixes
- Performance improvements
- Feature improvements

Otherwise, most other types of contributions we suggest opening an issue first to discuss if the feature/content fits within the vision of Balatro Multiplayer, including:
- New Content (Cards, Blinds, Gamemodes, etc)
- New Feature
- New or Replacement Art, if you aren't the original author we do not replace art

You are always welcome to use the MultiplayerAPI for your own standalone Multiplayer or Multiplayer extension mod.

## Contribution Guidelines

You are welcome to use AI as a tool in your contributions, but:
1. Communicate with us yourself, it is fine if you write the code with AI but I don't want to read your PR written entirely by Claude that is 100 lines when it could be 4.
2. Ensure the contributions are well tested and let us know how you tested the feature and how we can reproduce what you did.
